## R1 — bounded reproducibility metadata

- Objective: make benchmark manifests genuinely typed, bounded and immutable;
  share validation with new ledger appends rather than two drifting schemas.
- Files: `pipeline/benchmark.py`, `pipeline/experiment_ledger.py`,
  `tests/test_benchmark.py`, `tests/test_experiment_ledger.py`.
- Prerequisite: existing metadata-only manifest and explicit ledger append path.
  Independent of SQLite ownership and installed-provider smoke outcomes.
- Security: reject unsupported versions/fields, private-shaped hardware keys,
  nested/non-finite values, invalid UTF-8/control characters and invalid numeric
  types; metadata labels are caller declarations, not attestation or proof they
  contain no secrets. Do not rewrite or retroactively trust old ledger records.
- Tests: invalid metadata, retained input mutation, serialization copies,
  strict round trips, append-before-I/O rejection and old valid shape compatibility.
- Status: implemented; 10 manifest/plan and two new append-boundary cases pass.
  Full native regression passes. No live smoke required; WSL regression pending.
- Deferred: actual fixture/hardware measurement, new manifest schema, sensitive
  opt-in logging, concurrent ledger integrity and historical-data migration.

## R2 — controlled model-comparison eligibility

- Objective: a pure fail-closed guard for fixed-workload model scaling. Only

- Files: `pipeline/benchmark_comparison.py`, `tests/test_benchmark_comparison.py`.

- Deferred: ablations, human studies, significance/variance calculations,

## R3 — finite and valid ledger measurements

- Objective: prevent malformed numbers from entering new research result records.
- Files: `pipeline/experiment_ledger.py`, `tests/test_experiment_ledger.py`.
- Prerequisite: existing bounded result schema; independent of runtime/platform
  smokes. Does not change measurements or perform a production ledger append.
- Security: numeric values finite with magnitude at most 10^15; rates [0, 1],
  count metrics nonnegative integers and durations nonnegative. Strict JSON
  serialization occurs before creating directories/appending. No raw values
  in numeric rejection text. This is validation, not attestation or concurrency.
- Tests: three new cases cover NaN/infinity, invalid rates/counts/durations,
  extreme integers, nested retrieval score rejection, no-file failures and valid
  value preservation. The complete ledger suite passes 20 tests.
- Status: implemented; full native regression passes; same WSL gate only.
- Deferred: historical read validation/migration, concurrent append integrity,
  metric collection, statistical interpretation and research export.
