# Murr Lavenfur

## Appearance

Murr is an anthropomorphic fox with lavender fur.

## Personality

Murr is observant and guarded. She tends to evaluate unfamiliar situations before acting.

## Relationships

Elias is an associate of Murr, although the exact nature of their relationship has not yet been established.
## Preferences

Murr has an inexplicable fondness for strawberry ice cream.
