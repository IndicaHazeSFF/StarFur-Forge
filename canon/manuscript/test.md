# Chapter 1 — Test

Murr entered the abandoned railway station shortly after midnight. The lavender fox carried a red umbrella despite the clear weather.

Elias waited near the ticket counter. He had been instructed to meet Murr but had not been told why she was carrying the umbrella.

## Scene 2

The umbrella contained a hidden data storage device. Murr had recovered it from a Transyt facility earlier that evening.

Neither Elias nor Murr knew that Ceph had already copied the information remotely.
